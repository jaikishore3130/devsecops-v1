# Phase 7B - Future Incident Prediction Modeling

## Scope

Future incident prediction only on the frozen Phase 7A dataset `data/processed/prediction_v2/`. No RCA, remediation, autonomous execution, cybersecurity task, real-world retraining, or Phase 8 work was performed.

## Formal Prediction Definition

Observation window W = 900s; prediction horizon H = 900s; target interval `(prediction_timestamp, prediction_timestamp + 900s]`; target `future_incident_label`.

## Negative-Control Comparison

Phase 7 used independent future events, so the target was statistically independent of pre-t telemetry (ROC-AUC ~0.50, PR-AUC ~0.061). Phase 7A introduced a latent scenario process that drives both noisy pre-t warning telemetry and a probabilistic future event. Phase 7B trains models on that corrected signal. Phase 7 and Phase 7A datasets were never combined or retrained against each other.

## Dataset and Freeze

Train = 1,000,000; validation = 50,000; test = 50,000. All three SHA-256 hashes match the frozen Phase 7A manifest. train=57f8fc0156fd6ac244c46043958463f12fb954ccd2174dccf9330e6b95de0ba6, validation=02d8bec033dc03941e7291cbc014dedc122fb32364547b9d51ff8a2b55d476be, test=c737990db0e256f116904d4bd1151ea10c62be9bae9b9be202e1a4106188306d.

## Feature Contract

76 approved SAFE_FEATURE fields were used. Excluded: future target/type/time, lead time, latent_scenario_state, current incident label/type, identifiers, RCA, remediation, and post-event/UNSAFE fields. Missing indicators are generated by the train-fitted imputer.
Note: `anomaly_score`, `cpu_delta`, `memory_slope`, `failure_rate`, `retry_rate`, `burst_score`, and the rolling request-rate features are pre-cutoff derived features and are part of the safe contract; they are removed only in the SAFE_FEATURES_WITHOUT_DERIVED_FEATURES ablation.

## Preprocessing

Numeric: train-fitted median imputation with missing indicators, then StandardScaler. Categorical (environment, region, protocol, service): train-fitted most-frequent imputation and unknown-safe one-hot encoding. Fitted on train only; the frozen transform is applied to validation and test.

## Generalization / Entity Isolation

Features contain no entity identifiers; the Phase 7A splits are entity-isolated. Performance therefore reflects telemetry dynamics rather than entity memorization. The split was not changed after observing test performance.

## Limitations

All results are on a synthetic development dataset. The latent scenario mechanism is a research design, not externally validated causal evidence. ESCALATION has small support and is reported with that limitation. No real-world generalization claim is made, and no Phase 8 work was started.
